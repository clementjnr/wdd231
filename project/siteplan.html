SkyRoute Travels - Website Site Plan

Site Name

SkyRoute Travels

This name represents a travel agency that helps people discover destinations, book flights, compare travel packages, and receive travel guidance. The name is simple, memorable, and reflects the purpose of the website.

Site Purpose

SkyRoute Travels provides visitors with information about popular travel destinations, travel packages, airline recommendations, and travel planning services. Users can explore destinations, compare packages, and contact the agency for travel assistance.

Scenarios

  • What travel packages are available for Dubai and London?
  • How can I contact a travel consultant to help plan my vacation?

Color Scheme

  • Blue (#0056B3) - Header, navigation, buttons, headings.
  • Light Gray (#F4F7FA) - Background color and content sections.
  • Orange (#FF9800) - Buttons, icons and highlighted links.

Typography

  • Poppins - Main headings and navigation.
  • Open Sans - Paragraphs and general content.
